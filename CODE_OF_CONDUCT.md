# Code of Conduct

RustUse is committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional.
- Focus discussion on code, docs, APIs, and project direction.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while keeping a high bar for quality.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, intimidation, or intentionally disruptive conduct.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted advances.

## Scope

This applies to RustUse repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the relevant
repository maintainers privately when possible. If no private channel is
available, use GitHub's reporting or moderation tools.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, lock, or moderate content that violates this code
of conduct and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.
